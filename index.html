"This isn't a diet book. It's the protocol I wish someone had handed me on day one — in order, with room for when it doesn't go the way the chapter says it will."

Owen Archer writes practical, structured protocols for people managing autoimmune illness through diet — built around elimination and reintroduction, not a single miracle food. His first book, The Autoimmune Carnivore Protocol, walks readers through a 16-chapter, phase-by-phase process for testing what their own body can and can't tolerate.

He also builds the free printable tools that go with each book — checklists, trackers, and worksheets meant to be lived with, not just read once. This is his author site, home to his books and the resources written to go alongside them.
